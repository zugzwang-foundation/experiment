## D-26 · D-21 is narrowed to the conference sense

**Narrows** D-21 (2026-09-04)

D-21 said *"no document references Devcon, ETHGlobal, a conference, a showcase, or the repository
archive."* Read literally that strikes shipped product surfaces: `SPEC.1` §21.4 is a named
**Historical-debate showcase** with its own architecture, `"Showcase"` is a resolver-card label in
`src/components/debate/`, and *"press conferences"* appears inside a market's resolution criteria.
Fifty-three raw hits, roughly twenty-four of them ordinary vocabulary.

**Ruling.** D-21 strikes **the event, not the words**. Specifically:

| Struck | Kept |
|---|---|
| Devcon, ETHGlobal, and any named conference or venue | *"showcase"* and *"conference"* as product or market vocabulary |
| The 6–8 November window and any date justified by it | `SPEC.1` §21.4, the resolver-card labels, and their tests |
| The repository-archive boundary (`2026-11-08`), 24 sites | Market resolution criteria — founder content, never swept |
| Post-experiment activity of any kind | The dataset release, and the freeze instant `2026-11-05 23:59 UTC` |

**The test.** A site is struck if deleting it removes a claim about what happens **after
2026-11-05**. If it removes a product noun, it is not a D-21 site.
