1. What this is

The Zugzwang Experiment ("the Service") is an independent,
non-commercial research experiment run by an individual operator ("the
Operator", "we"). It is a prediction market in which participants take
positions on real-world questions using a fictional score, and every
position must carry a written argument.

The experiment is live from 15 September 2026 to 5 November 2026
(23:59 UTC), after which it ends permanently: markets resolve, the
platform stops accepting activity, and the experiment's pseudonymous
activity record becomes part of a published open research record.

Nothing on the Service involves real money. Participation is free.

3. Your identity on the Service

When you sign up, you are automatically assigned a pseudonym (for

are permanent: they cannot be chosen, changed, or transferred, and

Your pseudonym is public and your activity is recorded as a permanent

Keep your account to yourself. Do not share access to it, do not

5. Markets and resolution

Markets are questions about real-world outcomes. Every position you
take must include a written argument — on this Service there is no
stake without a voice, and replies to arguments are themselves
positions.

All markets resolve when the experiment concludes. The Operator is the
sole resolver of every market: the Operator determines outcomes in
good faith against each market's stated resolution criteria, may
correct a resolution made in error, and may void a market that becomes
unresolvable. The Operator's determinations are final. The Operator
does not participate in any market.
