Zugzwang Experiment — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy sits alongside the Terms of Service published with
it. Terms defined there carry the same meaning here.


1. What we collect, and why

We collect:

  — your email address, to create your account and send sign-in codes;
  — if you sign in with Google, your Google account identifier,
    display name, and avatar image, to authenticate you — these are
    never shown publicly;
  — technical data such as IP address and browser user-agent, for
    security, abuse prevention, and to record your acceptance of these
    documents;
  — your activity on the Service — positions, arguments, uploads, and
    their timestamps, which are the experiment itself.

We use this data to operate the Service, keep it secure, understand
aggregate usage, and produce the research record. We do not sell your
data, and we do not use it for advertising.


2. Who else processes it

The Service runs on infrastructure operated by service providers, who
process data on the Operator's behalf: Vercel (hosting), Supabase
(database), Cloudflare (file storage and bot protection), Upstash
(rate limiting), Resend (sign-in emails), Sentry (error monitoring),
PostHog (usage analytics), and OpenAI (automated content screening).

Some of these providers operate systems outside your country, so data
may be processed across borders. Each provider receives only what its
function requires.


3. Cookies

The Service uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies: a
session cookie that keeps you signed in, and security tokens that
protect the Service from automated abuse. Usage analytics are
collected in aggregate to understand how the Service is used. We use
no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking.


4. Retention, and what happens at the end

The Service's record of activity — positions, arguments, and the
ledger connecting them — is append-only by design: it is a permanent
record that is not edited or deleted, because the integrity of that
record is the point of the experiment.

When the experiment ends on 5 November 2026, all markets resolve, all
Dharma is void, and the Service stops accepting activity. The activity
record is then prepared for publication: direct identifiers — your
email address, Google account details, IP addresses, and browser data
— are removed and are never published. What is published is the
pseudonymous record: pseudonyms, positions, arguments, and timestamps.

You may ask us at any time to delete the personal identifiers
associated with your account (your email and sign-in details). Because
the activity record is permanent and pseudonymous, published arguments
and positions cannot be deleted.


5. Contact

For questions about this Policy, or any request concerning your data,
write to legal@zugzwangworld.com. Requests are reviewed promptly, and
we act on valid ones.
